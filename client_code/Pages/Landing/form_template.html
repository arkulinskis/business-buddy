My business isn't in the list. Can I still use it?

Yes: the Basics and the permit finder apply to every business. And tell us which guide you'd like next with the feedback button.

Who can see my plans?

Only you. Your plans, notes and budgets are private. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.

What about the suburbs or the rest of Québec?

Provincial rules (registration, taxes, MAPAQ, RBQ…) are the same everywhere in Québec. Municipal permits are Montréal-specific here; other cities work similarly but with their own by-laws.
